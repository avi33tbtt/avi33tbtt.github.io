Data-fidelity note: the paper reports 130 pooled cases almost entirely as aggregate frequencies (Tables 1–5). True individual, linked patient trajectories are only available for the 6 fatal autopsy cases in Table 6. Those 6 are rendered here as real threaded patient paths. The rest of the network (grey/blue "population" nodes) represents sequential clinical checkpoints weighted by their reported marginal prevalence across the 130-case cohort — it is not a verified joint trajectory for each of the other 124 patients, since the paper's individual-level supplementary file (S2) was not part of the source document provided.
