RCT Trajectory Mapper

Design: Parallel-group RCT (CONSORT)
Design classification. Parallel-group randomized controlled trial, 16 weeks, 1:1 allocation via computer-generated sequence with opaque sealed envelopes; CONSORT 2010 guidelines followed and a CONSORT diagram (Fig. 1) reported — meets Module E (RCT / CONSORT trajectory) criteria. Intervention group (IG, n=15) received individual dietary consultations at weeks 0/4/8 plus weekly remote support; control group (CG, n=15) received usual diet with basic advice only at study end. Per-protocol analysis on the 28 completers (14/arm) is the primary evidence base (Jayawardena et al., J Int Soc Sports Nutr 2026;23(1):2683613).
Hub / textbook-path summary

The modal IG trajectory: baseline → 3 in-person consults (wk 0,4,8) + weekly WhatsApp support (wk 10-16) → reduced fat mass and increased fat-free mass and handgrip strength at week 16, all with p<0.05 and medium-large effect sizes (d=0.33-2.04). The modal CG trajectory: baseline → no structured advice → essentially flat or slightly worsening body composition (all CG changes non-significant, p>0.05). These are the two "textbook paths" a two-arm RCT naturally produces (Table 2, Jayawardena et al. 2026).

Outlier / divergence summary
NodeWhy notable
FFM (BIA) trajectory reversalIG gained FFM (+4.28kg) while CG lost FFM (-1.08kg) over the same 16 weeks — an opposite-direction split, the strongest signal in the whole dataset (between-group p<0.001), consistent with the paper's RED-S caution that unmonitored diets can erode lean mass (Discussion, refs [9,10,51]).
UAFA (upper-arm fat area)Largest effect size in the paper (d=2.04) — outlier-magnitude change relative to all other anthropometric nodes.
MUAC decreasing while FFM(DEXA) increasingApparent paradox (arm circumference shrank while whole-body FFM grew) — reflects fat loss from the upper arm outpacing lean gain there, a node worth flagging rather than averaging away.
