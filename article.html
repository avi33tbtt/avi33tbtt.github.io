Article Index: From First Principles to a Framework You'll Actually Use

Four pieces, read in order, tracing the same idea — Promption and Provocation as cognitive modes for studying clinical cases — from a first working demonstration, through progressively heavier ground-truth tooling, to a deliberate step back toward something small enough to use every week.

Tooling, complexity, and claimed accuracy rise through Parts 1–3, then the series deliberately turns back toward usability in Part 4 — because a framework only teaches you something if you actually open it.
