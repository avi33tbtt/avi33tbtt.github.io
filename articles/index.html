Reasoning-Validity vs. Patient-Validity: Six Articles, One Thread

A short summary of what each piece teaches, in the order that builds the argument.

Five of these six articles form a single connected chain — a foundational distinction that gets repackaged, operationalized into a framework, then audited against the real VibeRounds build. The sixth is an adjacent project under the same umbrella that doesn't extend the chain, but is worth knowing about.

Core thread
01 · Foundation

Good for Self-Audit of Reasoning, Still Not a Way to Decide a Patient's Care

The piece everything else builds on. Walks through the full clinical-AI verification stack — pathway grounding, knowledge graphs, RAG, GraphRAG, neuro-symbolic validation — and shows each layer checking a claim against a reference, never checking whether the reference is the right frame for the patient.

What you'll learn: the core distinction between reasoning-validity (is this claim consistent with a reference) and patient-validity (is the reference the right frame right now) — and why the gap is a category boundary, not something better tooling closes.
avi33tbtt.github.io/articles/thinking-process.html 02 · Reframe

Trivial Tasks vs. Deep Reasoning in Clinical AI

A tighter read on Article 01, splitting the same argument into two clean buckets and adding a side-by-side comparison table.

What you'll learn: why bounded checks (dosing, interactions, guideline citations) are a reliability problem that keeps shrinking, while noticing which detail should redirect a case is a category boundary that doesn't.
avi33tbtt.github.io/articles/healthcare-trivial-vs-deep-reasoning.html 03 · Operationalize

Best of Both Worlds: Augmenting Clinical Training Without Pretending to Replace Noticing

Follows directly from Article 02's table, turning the two-bucket split into a four-job architecture — each job with its own honest scope and its own forbidden claim.

What you'll learn: the four jobs — trivial-task automation, post-hoc audit, case-based sandbox, longitudinal analytics — and why asking one system to do all four is the design mistake, not under-building the tooling.
avi33tbtt.github.io/articles/clinical-ai-best-of-both-worlds.html 04 · Audit reality

VibeRounds — Paradigm Coverage of the Four Tasks

Takes the four-job framework from Article 03 and checks it against what VibeRounds' actual components — Socratic Learning, Guided Discovery, Analytics Mode, the 57+ module toolset, and the Research layer — really cover today.

What you'll learn: a task-by-task coverage matrix showing where VibeRounds has strong, purpose-built coverage (the sandbox), where it's partial, and where the gap is total and named openly (bounded automation).
avi33tbtt.github.io/articles/viberounds-coverage-matrix.html 05 · Build protocol

Post-Hoc Clinical AI Audit — Staged Decision Framework

An interactive six-stage decision tool for actually composing a rigorous audit protocol, rather than assuming one. Cross-references Job 03 from the coverage matrix and re-runs the same "where VibeRounds actually lands" reality check from Article 04.

What you'll learn: how persona and audit-type (Stage 0) license every downstream choice — scenario generation, query formulation, answer arms, audit method, and blinded scoring — and why a learner's sandbox and an auditor's compliance check should never share a rubric.

Adjacent project
Adjacent · Not part of the core chain

Person-Centered Clinical Analytics: A Walkthrough for Medical Learners and Analysts

A separate, self-contained demo (synthetic T1D vs. T2D patient data) built around a different question: can one disease-agnostic graph schema — Observations → Diagnosis → Interventions → Outcomes — represent any patient, without disease-specific branching in the code.

What you'll learn: how to stress-test a data model for hidden special-casing, why a sticky confounders banner matters more than the numbers themselves, and how cohort-level trajectory maps complement single-patient views. Doesn't extend the reasoning-validity/patient-validity thread — shares only the family discipline of being honest about what a tool can't claim.
