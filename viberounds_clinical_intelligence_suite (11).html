VIBEROUNDS · CLINICAL INTELLIGENCE SUITE — FHIR COHORT DASHBOARD

One cohort, mapped as trajectories, then twenty-two analytics — live in your browser

A synthetic 500-patient cohort is generated on load, first walked through a person-centered trajectory map (the same six checkpoints for every diagnosis, hub nodes emerging from convergence — not averaged), then run through twenty-two deterministic analytic passes below it — organized to cover every clinical, public-health, patient-centered, and evidence-based analytic type in the VibeRounds analytics catalog. No server, no fetch. Regenerate the cohort to see the whole dashboard re-run on a fresh sample, or jump to the full analytics catalog.

Data source

Credentials are used only for in-browser requests to the URL you provide and are never sent anywhere else. CORS must be enabled on your server for browser-based querying to work — if it isn't, query it through a proxy you control.
