# VibeRounds — Junior Resident Combo

## Who this is for
PGY-2/3 residents who've moved past illness-script acquisition and are now running cases with real autonomy. The focus shifts to **reasoning quality**: tightening problem representation, catching your own bias, and starting to use probability explicitly instead of gut feel alone.

## Why these modules
This tier adds the analytic layer interns skip — semantic qualifiers, causal vs. probabilistic reasoning, Bayesian updating, bias auditing — while keeping one foot in the live-case-audit workflow you're now responsible for.

## The Combo (9 modules)

| Order | Module | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Module 5 — Real-Time Case Review & Data Audit](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-05-Real-Time-Case-Review-and-Data-Audit.html) | You now own live case logs, not just presentation prep |
| 2 | [Module 17 — Semantic Qualifiers & Problem Representation](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-17-Semantic-Qualifiers-Problem-Representation.html) | Tightens the one-liner — the skill that separates PGY-1 from PGY-2 presentations |
| 3 | [Module 12 — Differential Diagnosis Deepdive](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-12-Differential-Diagnosis-Deepdive.html) | Widens and prioritizes the differential beyond the reflex list |

| 5 | [Module 36 — Bayesian Probability / Likelihood Ratio Engine](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-36-Bayesian-Probability-Likelihood-Ratio-Engine.html) | Makes pre/post-test reasoning explicit instead of implicit |

| 8 | [Module 50 — Diagnostic Reasoning Map (DRM)](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-50-DRM-Diagnostic-Reasoning-Map.html) | Externalizes your reasoning chain so it can be checked |

## Suggested workflow
On admission: **5 → 17 → 12**. Mid-workup: **18 → 36 → 33** as new data lands. Before presenting to the senior/attending: **26 → 50 → 53** as a self-check pass.
