# VibeRounds — Attending Combo

## Who this is for
Attendings who are teaching, generating evidence from their own case series, and making comparative-effectiveness or economic calls — the modules where the "learner" using the prompt is the one training everyone else.

## Why these modules
This tier is the only one that pulls from **Framework Architecture & Meta-Design** (designing/reviewing new Socratic prompts) and **Medical Education & Literature** categories, plus the research and health-economics modules that don't make sense until you have a panel of cases to draw on.

## The Combo (10 modules)

| Order | Module | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Module 8 — Socratic-Mode Design Specification](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-08-Socratic-Mode-Design-Specification.html) | For writing or vetting new teaching prompts for your own service |
| 2 | [Module 9 — N-of-1 Case Research Protocol](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-09-Case-Research_Protocol.html) | Turns your unusual cases into a structured seven-stage report |
| 3 | [Module 7 — Longitudinal & Cross-Case Learning](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-07-Longitudinal-and-Cross-Case-Learning.html) | Mines your registry across cases and time, not one case at a time |
| 4 | [Module 10 — Journal & Article Reading](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-10-Medical-Journal-Article-Reading.html) | Fast, structured critical read for journal club or self-teaching |
| 5 | [Module 21 — Evidence Frontier Search](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-21-Evidence-Frontier-Search.html) | Finds where the evidence actually thins out on a given question |
| 6 | [Module 46 — Evidence-Based Medicine Insights](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-45-Evidence-Based-Medicine-Insights.html) | Structured critical appraisal you can teach directly from |
| 7 | [Module 48 — Treatment Comparative Analysis & Evidence-Informed Prognosis Trajectory](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-48-Treatment-Comparative-Analysis-and-Prognosis-Trajectory.html) | Head-to-head treatment/prognosis calls at the attending level |
| 8 | [Module 43 — Health Economics & Value-Based Care Alignment](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-43-Health-Economics-Value-Based-Care-Alignment.html) | Cost/value framing that's now part of your decision-making |
| 9 | [Module 53 — Clinical Guideline Intelligence Navigator](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-53-Clinical-Guideline-Intelligence-Navigator.html) | Keeps your own practice and your team's guideline-current |
| 10 | [Framework D — Critical Awareness Framework](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Framework-D-Critical-Awareness-Framework.html) | The biases and legitimate critiques of AI-assisted reasoning itself — worth holding as you set standards for everyone below you |

## Suggested workflow
Teaching-prep: **8 → 54-style question sets (borrowed from the senior tier) → Framework D** as a standing lens. Evidence work: **10 → 21 → 46 → 48 → 43** per question or per case series. Research pipeline: **9 → 7** for cases worth writing up.
