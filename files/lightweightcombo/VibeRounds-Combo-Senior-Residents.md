# VibeRounds — Senior Resident Combo

## Who this is for
PGY-4+ residents and chiefs who are running teams, owning complex/unstable patients, and starting to think in systems — throughput, failure modes, and teaching — rather than single-case reasoning alone.

## Why these modules
This tier trades single-case differential-building for **team-level and systems-level cognition**: recognition-primed decisions under time pressure, pre-mortems on your own plans, throughput and workflow strategy, and a question-generator you can hand to juniors.

## The Combo (9 modules)

| Order | Module | Why it's here |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Module 20 — Naturalistic Decision Making (Recognition-Primed Decision Model)](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-20-Recognition-Primed-Decision-Model.html) | Formalizes the pattern-match-under-pressure calls you now make constantly |
| 2 | [Module 42 — Clinical Pre-Mortem](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-42-Clinical-Pre-Mortem.html) | Stress-tests your own plan before it fails, not after |
| 3 | [Module 38 — Poly-Crisis & Cascading Failure Simulator](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-38-Poly-Crisis-Cascading-Failure-Simulator.html) | For the multi-system-failure patients that land on your service |
| 4 | [Module 49 — FMEA Analysis](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-49-FMEA-Analysis-and-Insights.html) | Structured failure-mode audit for a case or a process |
| 5 | [Module 34 — The High-Value Care (HVC) Auditor](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-34-High-Value-Care-Auditor.html) | You're now accountable for utilization, not just accuracy |
| 6 | [Module 40 — The Operational & Throughput Strategist](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-40-Operational-Throughput-Strategist.html) | Ward/team throughput is now partly your job |
| 7 | [Module 41 — Clinical Workflow Implementation Science](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-41-Clinical-Workflow-Implementation-Science.html) | For actually landing a process change on the ward, not just proposing one |
| 8 | [Module 51 — Systems-Based Clinical Analysis](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-51-Systems-Based-Clinical-Analysis-and-Insights.html) | Zooms a single case out to the system it happened in |
| 9 | [Module 54 — System 1 & System 2 Thinking Question Generator](https://avi33tbtt.github.io/Prompts/Module-54-System1-System2-Question-Generator.html) | The teaching tool you hand juniors on rounds |

## Suggested workflow
Case-level: **20 → 38 (if applicable) → 42**. Team/system-level, run periodically rather than per-case: **34 → 40 → 41 → 51**. On teaching rounds: **54** to generate questions for juniors, with **49** as a periodic safety review.
