What Varies by Setting

  • Core evidence-based, setting-independent: ECG/cardiac exclusion, consistent PPI use, dietary trial, psychotherapy referral
  • Resource-dependent: Esophageal manometry/pH-impedance, PFTs with DLCO, and CT chest may not be available in resource-limited settings — in that case, a therapeutic PPI trial plus empiric anxiety management with close follow-up substitutes for invasive/expensive testing
  • Access to gut-directed hypnotherapy and CBT is highly variable globally; telehealth psychotherapy or structured self-help CBT materials are a lower-resource substitute

  • Why each test: The scope checks your food pipe for damage; the lung tests check how your lungs work after old TB; blood tests check for deficiencies and thyroid issues that can mimic anxiety symptoms.
