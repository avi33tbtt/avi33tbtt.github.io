Healthcare Debate Bench

Put a clinical case, a health-policy question, or a hospital dilemma in front of a panel of the healthcare system's own stakeholders — physicians, nurses, allied health, administrators, patients' advocates, policymakers, and medical trainees. Each persona argues strictly from its own seat in the system — none of them soften for the others.
Bring your own key. Your API key is stored only in this browser's local storage and sent directly from your browser to the provider — never through any server of ours. This is a reasoning and rhetoric exercise for medical education and health-policy discussion, not a source of clinical, diagnostic, or professional medical advice.
