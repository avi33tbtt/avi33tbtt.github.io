Insights Summary

  • Individual → Comparative Reading cases one-by-one (Atlas) surfaces very different insights than reading them nested and cross-compared (Analysis) — the same data tells a different story depending on the lens.
  • Decision Points A small number of recurring decision nodes — timing of referral, RAAS blockade titration, dialysis-start conversations — account for a disproportionate share of divergent outcomes across the cohort.
  • Staging ≠ Trajectory Two patients with the same eGFR/staging snapshot can be on very different trajectories; the decision-node view is where that distinction becomes visible.
  • Purpose This suite is a demonstration of moving from individual case review to structured comparative and decision analysis — a workflow pattern, not a validated clinical tool.
