**The reasoning suffered from "Diagnostic Anchoring via Unbundled Symptoms."**

The audit revealed that the initial provider coupled three clinical signs (JVP, edema, dyspnea) into a single "heart failure package" and initiated therapy. The audit unmasked a tendency to force divergent clinical feedback back into an established initial narrative rather than using therapeutic non-response as a trigger to restart the diagnostic workup.
