Title: General medicine case 1

A 45 year old man presented to the OPD with chief complaints of swelling of both legs and shortness of breath from the past 2 months.

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:

Patient was apparently asymptomatic 4 months back. Then he complained of bilateral pedal edema which was gradually progressive, pitting type, below the knee. He also complained of shortness of breath (Grade 2) and pain in the legs. He went to the nearest medical care and was diagnosed with a kidney problem and started on medication.

6 days back patient presented to the casualty with swelling in the legs and shortness of breath, for which he was advised to undergo dialysis. 3 dialysis units have been done till 10/8/21.

HISTORY OF PAST ILLNESS:
- Known case of HTN for the past 1 year
- Not a known case of DM, TTS (thyroid), asthma, and epilepsy
- No history of surgeries or blood transfusions in the past

FAMILY HISTORY:
- Patient's father was a known case of BP (hypertension)
- Patient's mother was a known case of sugar (diabetes) and BP
- No history of CVA, CAD, asthma, or thyroid disorder in the family
