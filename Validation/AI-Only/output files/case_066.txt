Title: DRUG INDUCED HEPATITIS SECONDARY TO BORDERLINE LEPROMATOUS LEPROSY DAPSONE SYNDROME

31 year old male, photographer by occupation, came to the casualty with complaints of:
- Yellowish discolouration of sclera since 4 days
- Fever since 2 days, insidious onset, gradually progressive, subsided on medication
- C/o nausea and vomitings since 2 days, non-bilious and non-projectile, food particle as content; was taken to local hospital in view of vomitings

HISTORY OF PRESENT ILLNESS:

Patient was apparently asymptomatic 17 years back, then his mother observed a hypopigmented patch over the cheek and multiple hypopigmented patches all over the body; this was neglected, thought to be due to vitamin deficiency. 15 days back, mother observed a hypopigmented patch on the right arm and took him to a local hospital, where he was diagnosed with Hansen's (leprosy) and was started on treatment since then.

No complaints of shortness of breath, palpitations, headache, orthopnea, PND.
Non-alcoholic. Non-smoker.

ON EXAMINATION:
- Patient is conscious, coherent, cooperative
- Oriented to time, place and person
- Pallor ++
- Icterus ++
- No cyanosis, clubbing, or lymphadenopathy
- Febrile: 99.9 F
- BP: 130/80 mmHg
- PR: 83 bpm, regular, normal volume

SYSTEMIC EXAMINATION:
- CVS: S1 S2, no murmurs
- RS: NVBS+, no crepts
- P/A: mild splenomegaly+, BS+
- CNS:
  - Tone: UL right normal, left normal; LL right normal, left normal
  - Power: UL right 5/5, left 5/5; LL right 5/5, left 5/5
  - Reflexes: Biceps, Supinator, Triceps, Ankle, Knee — right 2+ each, plantar flexor; left 2+ each, plantar flexor
- Sensory:
  - Fine touch: present
  - Crude touch: present
  - Lost sensation over the hypopigmented lesion on lower limbs
  - Intact sensation over the lesions on forearm and neck (touch, pain and temperature intact over forearm/neck lesions; pain and touch lost over lower limb lesions)

DIAGNOSIS (working/differential):
- ? Wilson's disease with Hansen's
- ? Drug induced hepatitis

TREATMENT GIVEN:
1. Tab. Udiliv 300mg PO/BD
2. Tab. Rifaximine 550mg PO/BD
3. Syp. Lactulose 10ml PO/TID
4. Inj. Pantop 40mg IV/OD
5. Inj. Zofer 4mg IV/SOS
6. IVF NS and RL @ 50ml with 1 amp of Optineuron IV/OD
7. Monitor vitals 2nd hourly
