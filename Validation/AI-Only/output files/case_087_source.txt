Chief complaints:
24 year old male, labourer, came with chief complaints of:
- Decreased urine output since 10 days
- Shortness of breath since 10 days
- Pedal edema since 10 days
- Fever since 10 days

History of presenting illness:
Patient was apparently asymptomatic 3 years back. Then he developed decreased urine output for which he went to a local hospital; scan was done which revealed hydronephrosis.

10 days back he had trauma to the left little finger and dorsum of right foot, for which he went to a local hospital and dressing was done.

10 days back he developed shortness of breath, insidious in onset, gradually progressive (Grade 4), aggravated on walking, relieved on medication.

Pedal edema since 10 days, extending till ankle.

Fever since 10 days, continuous, not associated with chills and rigor.

In view of increased serum creatinine, dialysis was initiated yesterday.

Past history:
Not a known case of DM, HTN, Asthma, Epilepsy, BA, TB, CAD.
No past surgical history.
No previous blood transfusions.
