LONG CASE
=====================================================================

Chief complaints:
A 28 year old male came with chief complaints of sudden fall followed by weakness of both the lower limbs (paraplegia) and loss of hand grip 10 days back, associated with bowel and bladder incontinence.

History of present illness:
Patient was apparently asymptomatic 1 month back, following which he developed productive cough, low grade fever for which he underwent sputum studies and tested positive for AFB bacilli and started ATT - HRZE regimen, 2 tab according to weight/PO/OD.

He developed generalized weakness and myalgia 15 days back.

10 days back, patient got up from bed and went to open the door and suddenly fell down, with no loss of consciousness and no frothing. Following which his brother got him up and since then patient developed bowel and bladder incontinence.

Weakness in both upper and lower limbs:
Lower limbs - able to move toes of bilateral lower limbs; unable to turn in bed; unable to get from lying position; unable to walk; unable to lift legs off the bed.
Upper limbs - unable to take comb to head; unable to hold plate or glass, button or unbutton shirt; unable to lift bucket; unable to eat food; able to move fingers and lift hand till mouth.
Weakness is symmetrical and progressive. No diurnal variation in weakness. No breathlessness, no ptosis, no facial weakness, no difficulty in chewing.

Fall - no loss of consciousness, not associated with involuntary movements, tongue bite, or rolling up of eyes; not preceded by chest pain, palpitations, sweating; not associated with headache, giddiness, presyncope or visual disturbances.

Patient also complained of electric shock-like sensation all over the body; initially experienced shock-like pain only in the neck which radiated to bilateral upper limbs when neck is flexed (Lhermitte's-type description), and paresthesia and tingling sensation over hands.

No history of disorientation, abnormal behaviour, speech disturbances, loss of smell. Visual acuity and colour vision normal. No diplopia or eye deviation. No abnormal facial sensation, no difficulty in mastication, no facial asymmetry/drooling/eye closure difficulty. No hearing impairment, vertigo, tinnitus. No dysphagia, dysarthria, nasal regurgitation, hoarseness. No difficulty shrugging shoulders. No tongue deviation.

Sensory: able to feel clothes over body; able to feel hot and cold water during bath; electric shock-like sensation all over body; paresthesia and tingling over bilateral hands. Bowel and bladder incontinence present.

Past history:
Known case of TB since 1 month, on ATT - HRZE.
Not a known case of DM, HTN, Asthma, Epilepsy, Thyroid disorders, Stroke, CAD.

Personal history:
Diet - mixed; Appetite - normal; Sleep - adequate; Bowel and bladder incontinence since 1 week; No allergies; No addictions.

Family history:
Father is a known case of TB, used ATT for 2 years.

Diagnosis (as recorded by presenter):
Anatomical - cervical spinal cord
Functional - quadriparesis
Etiological - tuberculosis
Pathological - compressive extramedullary and extradural lesion

General examination:
Patient conscious, coherent, cooperative, oriented to time, place, person.
No pallor, icterus, cyanosis, clubbing, koilonychia, lymphadenopathy, edema.
Afebrile. PR 80 bpm. RR 16 cpm. BP 100/70 mmHg.
Height 165 cm, weight 45 kg, BMI 16.5 kg/m2.

Systemic examination - CNS:
Higher mental functions: conscious, oriented, cooperative, comfortable. Registration, calculation, comprehension, naming, writing/reading, repetition normal. No dysarthria. Cranial nerve exam normal. No signs of meningeal irritation.

Motor system examination:
Lower limbs extended at knee joint; upper limbs in mid-flexed position. Bulk symmetrical bilaterally (no wasting/hypertrophy).
Tone: UL - N/N; LL - increased/increased (Right/Left).
Power: UL - 4/5, 4/5; LL - 0/5, 0/5 (Right/Left).
Reflexes (Right/Left): Biceps 3+/3+; Triceps 3+/3+; Supinator 2+/2+; Knee 3+/3+; Ankle 3+/3+.
Plantar: extensor (bilateral, implied).

Sensory system examination:
Pin prick and touch sensations normal. Pain and temperature sensations normal.
Vibration sense lost over forehead, mastoid, clavicle, sternum, vertebral spine, tibial tuberosity, ankle.
Joint position sense at great toe and thumb absent.

Cerebellum:
No nystagmus, no intentional tremor. Finger-nose, finger-nose-finger, knee-shin tests could not be assessed due to quadriparesis. No hypotonia. No speech abnormality. No pendular knee jerk. No titubation.

CVS: S1, S2 heard. No thrills. No murmurs.
Respiratory system: Trachea central. Bilateral air entry present. Normal vesicular breath sounds. No added sounds.
Per abdomen: Soft, non-tender. Bowel sounds heard. Hernial orifices normal. No palpable masses.

Provisional diagnosis: Cervical myelopathy? Potts spine?

Investigations: [Images referenced in source - lab/imaging reports not transcribed as text in source page]

Treatment given:
1. Inj. Optineuron 1 Amp in 100 ml NS IV/OD
2. Inj. Thiamine 200 mg in 100 ml NS IV/TID
3. ATT - according to body weight, 2 tab PO/OD
4. BP/PR/SpO2/Temp charting

FINAL DIAGNOSIS: Quadriparesis secondary to infectious spondylitis of C4, C5, C6, C7 and D1 with epidural abscess at C5-C6 level.

Update: Surgical drainage of abscess performed at Osmania; getting discharged.

Outcome of intervention:
Patient regained control over bowel and bladder. Improvement in power and tone (now walking). Advised to continue ATT.

SHORT CASE 1
=====================================================================

A 32 year old male, autodriver, presented with pedal edema since 10 days and decreased urine output since 10 days.

HPI: Asymptomatic one year ago; developed diminution of vision, diagnosed with hypertension, on Tab. Arkamin and Tab. Telma H since 1 year. Developed pitting edema below knee since 1 month, weakness and backache since 1 month relieved on rest. Decreased urine output. Consumes alcohol (180 ml) rarely.

Past history: Known case of CKD on MHD since 1 month. Known case of HTN since 1 year. Not a known case of DM, CAD, Asthma, TB, Epilepsy.

Personal history: Single; occupation - daily labourer; diet mixed; appetite normal; bowels regular; micturition decreased; no known allergies; drinks alcohol rarely.

Family history: Brother is a known case of HTN.

Drug history: Tab. Arkamin and Tab. Telma H for HTN since 1 year.

General examination: Conscious, coherent, cooperative. Pulse 98 bpm, RR 18/min, BP 150/80 mmHg, afebrile, GRBS 127 mg%, SpO2 98% room air. No pallor, icterus, cyanosis, clubbing, lymphadenopathy. Pitting edema of feet below knee present. No malnutrition/dehydration.

Systemic examination: CVS - S1, S2 heard, no thrills/murmurs. Respiratory - vesicular breath sounds, trachea central, no wheeze/dyspnoea. Abdomen - obese, no tenderness/palpable mass/hernial orifices/free fluid, liver/spleen not palpable, bowel sounds heard. CNS - conscious, normal speech/gait, cranial nerves normal, sensory/motor normal. Reflexes (Right/Left, all +2): biceps, triceps, supinator, knee, ankle.

Investigations: USG, ECG, 2D ECHO, hemogram performed (images only in source, not transcribed).

Provisional diagnosis: CKD on MHD secondary to hypertensive nephropathy.
Diagnosis: Heart failure with preserved ejection fraction.
Plan: Renal transplantation; discussion ongoing regarding ABO compatibility.

Treatment: Fluid restriction <1L/day; salt restriction <2.4g/day; T. Lasix 40 mg PO/BD; SAM 4pm; T. Nicardia 20 mg PO/TID; T. Arkamine 0.1 mg PO/BD.

SHORT CASE 2
=====================================================================

80 year old female, sedentary lifestyle, diagnosed with hypertension 5 years back, on Telma H 40/12.5 mg since then. Symptomatic since August 2nd night: two episodes of loose stools (foul smelling, yellow, sticky, no tenesmus), three episodes of vomiting (non-bilious, non-projectile, food particles). Given eldoper capsule by daughter-in-law (pharmacist); stools subsided but developed fever with chills/rigor and vomiting next morning. Treated at Narketpally KIMS hospital with IV fluids, ORS, Sporolac, symptomatic treatment.

History: Daily water intake usually boiled Sagar water, but drank unboiled Sagar water on the day of onset; other family members drank filter water; no other community members who drank Sagar water became ill.

General examination: Conscious, coherent, cooperative, heavy built, moderately nourished. Hair thin and grey. Neck short with double chin. Truncal obesity. BP 90/60 mmHg, pulse 100 bpm, temperature 103°F, GRBS 126 mg/dL, RR 20/min, SpO2 96% room air. Pallor present. No cyanosis/clubbing/icterus/edema/lymphadenopathy. Tongue dry, skin turgor lost, capillary refill time 5 sec.

Systemic examination: CVS - S1, S2 heard, no thrills/murmurs. Respiratory - vesicular breath sounds, trachea central, no wheeze/dyspnoea. CNS - conscious, normal speech/gait, sensory/motor normal. Abdomen - obese, soft, non-tender, no palpable mass/organomegaly, hernial orifices normal, bowel sounds present.

Provisional diagnosis: Acute gastroenteritis with known case of hypertension.

Day 1 of admission: Five episodes of loose stools, three episodes of vomiting, fever with chills, temp 103°F.

Treatment given: IV fluids; Inj. Pantop 40 mg OD; Inj. Zofer 4 mg IV SOS; T. Dolo 650 mg TID; Inj. Monocef 1 g IV BD; Inj. Metrogyl 100 ml IV TID; T. Sporolac DS TID; Inj. Neomol 1 g IV SOS (if temp >101°F); ORS sachet in 1L water, 200 ml after each loose stool episode.

Investigations:
Hb - 8.8 g/dL
Total WBC count - 9100 cells/cumm
Neutrophils - 80%
Lymphocytes - 15%
PCV - 25.5% (decreased)
Platelet count - 1.76 lakhs/cumm
Serum Creatinine - 2.2 mg/dL
Blood Urea - 69 mg/dL
Sodium - 136 mEq/L
Potassium - 3.2 mEq/L
Chloride - 97 mEq/L
Total Bilirubin - 1.18 mg/dL
Direct Bilirubin - 0.36 mg/dL
SGOT (AST) - 24 IU/L
SGPT (ALT) - 17 IU/L
ALP - 180 IU/L
Total Proteins - 6.5 g/dL
Albumin - 3.4 g/dL

USG: Raised echogenicity with grade-1 renal parenchymal changes in bilateral kidneys.

Subsequent days: Frequency of loose stools increased (10-14 episodes/day); vomiting and fever subsided; same treatment continued; Inj. Optineuron and T. Redotil added. Stool microscopy revealed few inflammatory cells. Stool culture negative. Hanging drop test showed no motility of organisms.

Questions posed by presenter:
- Is stool culture helpful in management of acute gastroenteritis?
- When to start antibiotics in acute gastroenteritis?
