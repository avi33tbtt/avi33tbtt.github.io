DIAGNOSTIC REASONING MAP — Case_009 (66F, fever on/off + increased stool frequency + vomiting)
Module: DRM — Diagnostic Reasoning Map

NODE 1 — Presenting Complaint
  Finding: 66F, fever (on/off), increased stool frequency (3 episodes/day x2d, normal
  consistency, no blood/mucus), nausea and vomiting (10 episodes/day x2d, non-projectile,
  non-bilious, food particles, ~30min post-prandial)
  Decision Point: Yes — Type: Bifurcating
  Insight: The triad of fever + diarrhea (non-bloody) + high-frequency vomiting in an elderly
  patient should immediately activate two parallel tracks — acute gastroenteritis (infective,
  self-limited) vs. a systemic/pre-renal process where GI symptoms are secondary to volume loss
  or another driver — and the very high vomiting frequency (10/day) is disproportionate to
  typical uncomplicated gastroenteritis, which should have been flagged as atypical rather than
  taken at face value.

      being treated as coincidental — the case narrative does not show this being reasoned
      through.
  2b. No significant weight loss: Decision Point: Closing
      Insight: Lowers (but does not eliminate) concern for an occult malignant process as the

  2c. Malnourished on exam, obese abdomen shape (noted together): Decision Point: Missed
      Insight: "Looks malnourished" alongside an obese abdomen is an internally tension-holding

  2d. No known DM/HTN/TB/CAD/asthma/CVA/thyroid/epilepsy: Decision Point: No
      Insight: Reduces likelihood of several common confounders but does not narrow the acute

  3b. No pallor/icterus/cyanosis/clubbing/lymphadenopathy/pedal edema: Decision Point: Closing

  3c. Diffuse abdominal tenderness, no organomegaly, obese shape: Decision Point: Bifurcating

      enteritis, but the absence of localizing signs means a surgical abdomen or focal source
