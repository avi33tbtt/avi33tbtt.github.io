HEADLINE FINDING — ONE TRANSCRIPTION ERROR (not a fabrication, but a real
value inaccuracy)
================================================================
Node 3c's "Finding" line states: **"power 5/5 upper limbs bilaterally, 4/4
lower limbs bilaterally."** The source table actually reads "LL - 4/5 . 4/5"
(right and left lower limb power both 4/5, not 4/4 — "4/4" isn't even a
standard power-grading notation, since power is conventionally graded out
of 5). This appears to be a one-time transcription slip: every subsequent
reference to this finding in the same output (the 3c Insight text, Node 4c,
the "Unused/unclear results" line, and the Highest-Yield Decision Point
section) correctly says "4/5." So this is an isolated typo rather than a
systemic misreading, but it is a literal inaccuracy in the one place a
reader would look first (the Finding line itself), and is exactly the kind
of small numeric slip the fabrication check is meant to catch even when
it's clearly not an invented finding.
