HEADLINE FINDING — UNFLAGGED TIMELINE DISCREPANCY (not a fabrication, but
a material completeness/accuracy issue)
================================================================
The source's own header states explicitly: **"History and HOPI: (History
started 5months back- from October 2020)."** The DRM output never mentions
this figure anywhere. Instead, Node 1's "Finding" line asserts the
presentation was "eventually traced over ~2-3 weeks to malignant
hypertension" — a duration the DRM output appears to have inferred purely
from the narrative's internal sequence (menstrual bleeding → vision
blurring → grandmother's death, 2-3 day delay → ophthalmologist 1 →
ophthalmologist 2 → BP/urea-creatinine discovery), without cross-checking
against the source's own stated 5-month anchor.

Compounding this: the source also states, later in the same paragraph, that
after the central line was placed the patient "has taken dialysis in
guntur for 2 months and her blurring of vision was reduced... Being nearer
to home she was brought to Kim's for further workup and dialysis." This
two-month interim dialysis course in Guntur, prior to transfer to KIMS, is
**never mentioned anywhere in the DRM output** — Node 4/5 treat "central
line placement and initiation of dialysis" as essentially the narrative's
endpoint, without capturing that a further two months of dialysis and a
subsequent facility transfer occurred before the KIMS presentation this
case is nominally about.

Put together, these two omissions mean the DRM's own stated timeline
("~2-3 weeks") is likely a significant underestimate of the true elapsed
time (which, per the source's own header, is closer to 5 months), and the
DRM's diagnostic-delay narrative implicitly treats "BP finally discovered
→ dialysis started" as the case's ending point, when the source describes
a longer subsequent course (2 months of dialysis elsewhere, then transfer)
that isn't factored into the synthesis at all.

This is not a fabrication — no invented values or events are present — but
it is a more consequential omission than the completeness gaps flagged in
earlier cases (e.g., unmentioned smoking history), because it directly
affects the accuracy of the case's own central analytical thread (the
diagnostic-delay timeline) rather than being a side detail. Given that this
DRM output was otherwise notably careful about flagging internal timeline
inconsistencies in other cases (e.g., case_011, case_015), missing the
source's own explicit "5 months" anchor here is a real and somewhat
surprising gap.

FABRICATION CHECK (case-level)
================================================================
Fabrication flag: No.
No node invents a finding, value, or event absent from the source. All
directly quoted history items (menorrhagia, grandmother's death, the two
ophthalmology visits, BP 220/100, urea/creatinine "around 16," negative PMH,
exam findings) match the source precisely.

However, this case has a genuine and non-trivial completeness/accuracy
problem distinct from prior cases' minor omissions: the DRM output's own
stated case timeline ("~2-3 weeks") conflicts with the source's explicit
"5 months back" header, and the source's stated 2-month interim dialysis
course in Guntur (before transfer to KIMS) is never incorporated anywhere
in the output. Neither is an invented fact, but together they mean the
DRM's central "diagnostic delay" narrative rests on an inaccurate and
unflagged timeline — the kind of issue the protocol's fabrication check is
meant to catch even when phrased as an inference rather than a direct claim.
Recommend this case be considered for inclusion in the 25-case human-
reviewed subset given the severity of this specific issue, since it's a
useful test of whether human reviewers independently catch the same gap.
