================================================================
FABRICATION CHECK (case-level)
================================================================
Fabrication flag: No.
All quoted vitals, lab values, treatment-day details, and the final diagnosis
match the primary source exactly. No node introduces content absent from the
source. The alcohol-duration inconsistency in the source is correctly noted
as-is rather than silently resolved.

Two completeness gaps are worth flagging for the writeup (neither is a
fabrication, both are omissions of real source data):
  1. The source's documented 10-year smoking history is never mentioned
     anywhere in the DRM output.
  2. The source's internal inconsistency in pain-abdomen duration ("2 days"
     vs. "1 week") is not flagged, even though the DRM output applied that
     exact scrutiny to the alcohol-duration inconsistency elsewhere — an
     inconsistent standard within the same output.
These point to a possible generation pattern worth watching across the
sample: DRM outputs may reliably use the most salient/prominent version of a
detail (matching the case title or the most repeated figure) while sometimes
under-surfacing a secondary or less prominent contradicting detail in the
same source. Not a fabrication risk, but a completeness/thoroughness pattern
to track across further cases (Section 3's contamination-signal logic would
suggest checking whether this correlates with case familiarity).
