# Clinical Pathway: Inguinal Hernia
**Subject Domain:** Anatomy / General Surgery | **Prevalence Tier:** COMMON  
**Pathway ID:** CP-ANAT-INGHERN-01  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-21

---

## 1. Triage & Initial Assessment
* **Primary Symptoms:** Visible or palpable bulge in the groin region that becomes more prominent with coughing, standing, or straining; dragging sensation or aching pain in the groin or scrotum.
* **Red Flags / Warning Signs:** Irreducible bulge (Incarceration), severe sudden pain, overlying skin erythema, nausea, vomiting, or fever (Strangulation). **Strangulation is a surgical emergency.**
* **Vital Signs Monitoring:** Standard baseline vitals; monitor for tachycardia or fever if strangulation is suspected.

---

## 2. Diagnostic Workup
| Phase | Diagnostic Test | Expected Findings | Clinical Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary** | Physical Examination | Palpable mass during Valsalva maneuver; assess for reducibility | Establish clinical diagnosis and differentiate direct vs. indirect |
| **Secondary** | Groin Ultrasound | Hernial sac protruding through inguinal canal | Clarify ambiguous presentations or confirm occult hernias |
| **Tertiary** | Abdominal/Pelvic CT Scan | Bowel loop or fat in inguinal canal with/without obstruction signs | Evaluate for complications (obstruction, strangulation) or complex cases |

---

## 3. Acute & Maintenance Interventions

### Surgical Management
1. **Symptomatic/Strangulated Hernia:** Prompt surgical repair (Hernioplasty/Herniorrhaphy). Tension-free mesh repair is the standard of care.
2. **Surgical Approach:** Laparoscopic (TAPP or TEP) preferred for bilateral or recurrent hernias; Open repair (Lichtenstein) is a standard alternative for unilateral first-time hernias.

### Pharmacotherapy (Post-Operative)
1. **First-Line Analgesia:** Acetaminophen and NSAIDs for routine post-operative pain management.
2. **Second-Line Analgesia:** Short-course oral opioids (e.g., Tramadol) only for severe breakthrough pain.

### Non-Pharmacological Management
* **Watchful Waiting:** Appropriate for asymptomatic or minimally symptomatic, easily reducible hernias in patients with high surgical risk.
* **Lifestyle Modifications:** Smoking cessation to improve tissue healing, weight loss to reduce intra-abdominal pressure, and avoidance of heavy lifting.

---

## 4. Discharge & Monitoring Checklist
- [ ] Patient is ambulating independently and tolerating oral intake.
- [ ] Post-operative pain is adequately controlled on oral medications.
- [ ] Patient understands wound care instructions and signs of surgical site infection.
- [ ] Patient counseled on lifting restrictions (typically no heavy lifting for 4-6 weeks).
- [ ] Outpatient surgical follow-up appointment scheduled within 1 to 2 weeks.
