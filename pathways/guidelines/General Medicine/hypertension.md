# Clinical Pathway: Hypertension (HTN)
**Subject Domain:** Cardiology / Internal Medicine | **Prevalence Tier:** COMMON  
**Pathway ID:** CP-CARD-HTN-02  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-21

---

## 1. Risk Stratification & Goal Setting
* **Initial Assessment:** Validate diagnosis via ambulatory BP monitoring (ABPM) or at least two separate office visits.
* **Risk Stratification:** Calculate ASCVD risk score (10-year risk). Screen for target organ damage (TOD): Left Ventricular Hypertrophy (LVH), Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD), Retinopathy.
* **Treatment Targets:**
    * **General:** < 130/80 mmHg.
    * **High-Risk/Diabetes/CKD:** Targeted goal < 130/80 mmHg (ensure adherence to specific specialty guidelines).

---

## 2. Diagnostic Workup (Secondary Causes)
| Category | Investigation | Clinical Trigger |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Renal** | Serum Cr, GFR, Electrolytes, Urinalysis | Suspected CKD or Renovascular HTN |
| **Endocrine** | Plasma Aldosterone/Renin, TSH, Fasting Glucose | Resistant HTN or unexplained hypokalemia |
| **Cardiac** | EKG / Echocardiogram | Assess for LVH or strain patterns |

---

## 3. Tiered Pharmacotherapy Management
### Step 1: Initial Monotherapy / Combination
* **Primary Agents:** ACE inhibitors (ACEi) or Angiotensin II Receptor Blockers (ARBs), Calcium Channel Blockers (CCBs), or Thiazide-like diuretics.
* **Combination Strategy:** Start dual therapy (ACEi/ARB + CCB or Diuretic) for Stage 2 HTN (BP ≥ 140/90).

### Step 2: Titration & Escalation
* **Monitoring:** Review BP logs every 2–4 weeks after medication initiation or dose adjustment.
* **Refractory Cases:** If target not met after 3 months of triple therapy, screen for secondary hypertension and consider referral to a specialist.

### Step 3: Lifestyle Intervention (Foundational)
* **Sodium Reduction:** < 2,300 mg/day (ideally < 1,500 mg).
* **Dietary Pattern:** DASH diet (rich in fruits, vegetables, low-fat dairy).
* **Physical Activity:** 150 minutes/week of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise.

---

## 4. Long-term Follow-up & Safety
- [ ] **Adherence Check:** Assess for medication non-compliance at every visit.
- [ ] **Side Effect Screening:** Monitor for cough (ACEi), peripheral edema (CCB), or electrolyte imbalance (diuretics).
- [ ] **Annual Review:** Lipid panel, kidney function, and assessment for target organ damage progression.
