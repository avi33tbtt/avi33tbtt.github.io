Vibe Rounds in Healthcare

🩺 Introducing: VIBE ROUNDS

Vibe coding changed software. It's time medicine had its equivalent.

Vibe Rounds is a concept for AI-augmented clinical care — where a doctor commands a multimodal AI in natural language, at the bedside, in real time.

Speak. Point. Show. Get answers.

  • "What are the drug interactions for this patient?"
  • Point camera at a wound → instant analysis
  • "Draft my SOAP note" → done in 30 seconds
  • Differential diagnosis, evidence-linked, right now

Not AI replacing doctors.
AI giving doctors the cognitive bandwidth to be their best — at 3 AM, on the 12th hour of a shift, for every patient.

The best version of every clinician, at every hour, for every patient.
