60 Year Old Man with Pedal Edema and Breathlessness — Chronic Kidney Disease

Source case log: https://22bangarilaxmiraj.blogspot.com/2022/05/60-year-old-male-with-bilateral-pedal.html

Part 1 — Case Narrative

A 60-year-old man presented with bilateral pedal edema of five months' duration and breathlessness that had developed over the preceding six days. His edema had been present at the ankles for several months but became pitting in nature over the last week, and his breathlessness had progressed to grade 4 severity by the time of presentation. He had no known history of diabetes, asthma, hypertension, tuberculosis, or epilepsy.

On examination he was conscious, cooperative, and well oriented, with stable vitals (blood pressure 120/80 mmHg, pulse 100 beats per minute) and an unremarkable cardiovascular, respiratory, abdominal, and neurological examination on the documented findings. His presentation — longstanding pedal edema culminating in significant breathlessness — was consistent with fluid overload from underlying renal impairment, and after work-up he was diagnosed with chronic kidney disease. He was started on diuretics, a calcium channel blocker, iron and vitamin D supplementation, and gastric protection, with the underlying cause of his kidney disease and his subsequent clinical course not detailed further in the source record.
