56-Year-Old Man with Breathlessness, Anasarca, and Renal Failure

Source case log: https://06akhil.blogspot.com/2021/11/general-medicine-case-4.html

Case Narrative

A 56-year-old man presented to the emergency department with a four-month history of shortness of breath. He had been asymptomatic until then, when he developed bilateral lower-limb swelling, reduced urine output, abdominal distension, and scrotal swelling. The pedal edema began as grade 1 pitting swelling and progressed in stages — to grade 2 after ten days and grade 3 after a further fifteen days — starting in the left lower limb up to the ankle before extending to the right lower limb up to the thigh. He denied loose stools, burning micturition, vomiting, or hematuria.

His past history was notable for pulmonary tuberculosis treated with a full course of anti-tubercular therapy some two decades earlier, and a three-month history of hypertension on treatment. There was no history of diabetes, epilepsy, or prior surgery, and his family history was unremarkable.

On examination he was conscious, coherent, and cooperative, moderately built and nourished, without pallor, cyanosis, icterus, or lymphadenopathy. Vitals showed a temperature of 98.6°F, pulse 98/min, blood pressure 160/90 mmHg, respiratory rate 20/min, and SpO2 98%. Cardiac and respiratory examinations were normal; the abdomen was distended with no palpable liver or spleen.

Laboratory work-up showed markedly deranged renal function with a blood urea of 164 mg/dl and serum creatinine of 8.7 mg/dl, alongside a normocytic normochromic anemia (hemoglobin around 7.7–7.8 g/dl) with relative neutrophilia, and hypoalbuminemia with low total protein. Viral markers (HIV, hepatitis B and C) were negative. Ascitic fluid analysis showed a SAAG of 1.8 with a low ascitic albumin and a lymphocyte-predominant cell count, with no malignant cells on cytology. The overall picture was consistent with chronic renal failure with associated hypertension, and he was managed with hemodialysis — with pre- and post-dialysis weights of 67 kg and 63.5 kg respectively during one recorded session — together with a packed red cell transfusion for his anemia.
