# Share Your Vibe Rounds Experience

**Vibe Rounds is a paradigm, not a product.** It's a way of using conversational AI — as a Socratic mentor for clinical education, a guided-discovery partner for case-based research, or a layer of clinical decision support (CDSS). Anyone can pick up this approach, write their own prompts, and apply it to their own context.

If you've experimented with it — in any direction, at any stage — we want to hear about it.

## Who This Is For

- **🎓 Academics & Educators** — adapted the Socratic framework for a course, specialty, or assessment format
- **🩺 Specialists & Clinicians** — built a domain-tuned version for your field (psychiatry, surgery, paediatrics, nursing, pharmacy, etc.)
- **📚 Medical Students & Trainees** — used the guided-discovery / two-tier approach on a case of your own
- **🌍 Patients & Non-Medical Explorers** — explored health questions through a Socratic, question-led AI conversation

No background or affiliation required. Ideas, half-finished experiments, and "this didn't work" reports are just as welcome as polished pilots.

## What to Share

- Your prompt(s) — for clinical education, guided discovery, CDSS, or anything else built on this paradigm
- **Success stories** — what worked, and in what context
- **Challenges or failure modes** — where it broke down, gave bad guidance, or needed guardrails
- **Pilots, research, or implementation notes** — even informal or in-progress

## How to Share

📧 **Email:** [avi33tbtt@gmail.com](mailto:avi33tbtt@gmail.com?subject=Vibe%20Rounds%20Contribution)

Send your prompt text, write-up, or just a few honest notes — whatever stage it's at. Tell us briefly:

1. What did you build, and what (if anything) was it based on?
2. What worked — and what didn't?
3. Is this an idea, a pilot, an ongoing study, or already published?

With your permission, we'll feature contributions (with attribution, or anonymously if you prefer) to help the wider community learn from what's being tried.
